The problem

Customers only realize something is about to run out once it already has.

In Brazil's grocery e-commerce, between 72.5% and 73% of carts are abandoned (ECDB, 2025). Without consumption forecasting, purchasing becomes a reaction to a shortage that has already happened.

That last-minute purchase costs more for the customer and represents lost demand for retailers.

The solution

Forecasting restocking before the need becomes urgent.

Kaiza identifies household consumption and anticipates when a product is about to run out, connecting that need to a network of retail and delivery partners competing for the order.

1

Identification

Household sensing recognizes the product and tracks its consumption over time.

2

Anticipation

The restocking forecast arrives before the shortage, at the right moment to act.

3

Connection

The anticipated demand is routed to a network of retail and delivery partners.
